# PathFinder 🚀

**PathFinder** is an AI-powered resume scanner and analyzer designed to help job seekers optimize their resumes. By leveraging artificial intelligence, PathFinder evaluates your resume against industry standards, checks for keyword optimization, and provides actionable feedback to maximize your chances of landing an interview.
